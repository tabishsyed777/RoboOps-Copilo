# Line-02 conveyor operating procedure

Document ID: ROB-SOP-022 · Revision 2 · Fictional demo procedure

## Belt tracking warning

If the tracking sensor reports drift, stop the belt through the operator panel and inspect for debris around the rollers. Do not reach into the conveyor while it is energized. Use the designated local isolator and verify zero movement before clearing a jam. A persistent tracking warning requires a maintenance ticket; operators should not change roller alignment.

## Motor load

The training simulator normally shows motor load between 35% and 75%. A sustained reading above 85% or a sudden jump over 15 percentage points should trigger a controlled stop and inspection for a jam or excessive belt load. These are training thresholds for the fictional Line-02 cell only.

## Recovery

After debris is removed under the site lockout procedure, close the access guard, confirm the area is clear, and ask a supervisor to authorize a reduced-speed test. Log the sensor reading, cause, and corrective work. Never bypass an interlock to restore throughput.
